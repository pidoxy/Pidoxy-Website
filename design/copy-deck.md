## Research

- **H1:** Asking where intelligent systems break, and why.
- **Intro:** My research spans medical imaging, computer vision, self-supervised learning and retrieval-augmented systems. The common thread: judge a model by the decision it supports, not by how good its output looks.
- **Feature:** SharpXR: denoising without erasing the diagnosis. Low-dose pediatric X-rays are noisy, and denoisers that blur fine structure can hurt the diagnosis they were meant to help. SharpXR is judged by the downstream task, not pixel metrics.
- **Sections:** 01 — PUBLICATIONS · Papers / 02 — ACTIVITY · Milestones

## Paper page (template: When Retrieval Hurts)

- **Overview:** Retrieval-augmented generation is supposed to ground a model in evidence. This first-author paper measures when it does the opposite in chest X-ray report generation, and explains why.
- **The problem:** A retrieved case can look similar for the wrong reasons. When it does, its findings leak into a report for a different patient.
